## Share

Open the bookmark and choose **Share**. Pick who gets access:

- a **person** of your organisation;
- a **role**: everyone holding that role, now or later;
- **everyone** in your organisation.

Then pick the level (owner, editor, sharer or viewer) and, if the access
should end, an **expiry** date in the future. After that moment the share
no longer gives anything; it stays in the list until someone removes it.

A person you share with directly sees a notice in the portal.

## Who may share what

- **Owners** may give and take back every level.
- **Sharers** may pass on the **viewer** and **sharer** levels only. They
  cannot make anyone an editor or owner, and cannot take those levels away.
- **Editors** and **viewers** cannot share.

The same person, role or "everyone" can hold a given level on a bookmark
once; sharing it again is refused.

## See and remove shares

**Share** on a bookmark lists who has access to it. **Sharing** in the
navigation lists the shares of every bookmark you may share, and lets you
filter by person or role. Choose **Remove** next to a share to take the
access back.

To see your own access to a bookmark, open it: the actions you are allowed
are shown, together with the shares that give you that access.

## Everything for everyone

Administrators can give everyone in the organisation a level on **every**
bookmark at once (for example viewer on all bookmarks). Use **Sharing**,
choose **Everyone** and leave the bookmark empty. A share for everyone on
one bookmark stays on that bookmark.
