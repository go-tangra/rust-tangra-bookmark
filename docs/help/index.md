**Bookmarks** keeps web links for you and your organisation. A bookmark is
private to you until you share it; nobody outside your tenant ever sees it.

## Concepts

- **Bookmark**: a web address (`http` or `https`) with an optional title,
  description and up to 20 tags.
- **Owner**: whoever creates a bookmark owns it and may change, remove and
  share it.
- **Sharing**: you give a person, a role or everyone in your organisation
  one of four levels on a bookmark:

  | Level | Open | Change | Remove | Share |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Owner | yes | yes | yes | yes |
  | Editor | yes | yes | | |
  | Sharer | yes | | | yes |
  | Viewer | yes | | | |

- **Administrators** of your organisation (the `admin` and `owner` roles)
  can open and manage every bookmark of the organisation.
