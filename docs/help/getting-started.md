## Save a bookmark

Open **Bookmarks** and choose **New bookmark**. Enter the web address; it
must start with `http://` or `https://`. The title, description and tags
are optional. Tags are short words such as `docs` or `go`; spaces around a
tag are ignored and a tag given twice counts once.

You become the bookmark's **owner**.

## Find bookmarks

The list shows every bookmark you can open: your own and those shared with
you. Newest come first; sort by title, address or last change instead by
clicking a column.

- **Tags**: pick one or more tags to see only bookmarks carrying all of
  them.
- **Search**: type part of a title or address.

## Change or remove

Open a bookmark and choose **Edit** to change its address, title,
description or tags; you need the owner or editor level. Choosing new tags
replaces the old set.

**Remove** deletes the bookmark and every share of it. Only owners (and
administrators) can remove a bookmark, and it cannot be undone.

## When something is missing

A bookmark nobody shared with you does not appear anywhere, and opening its
link answers "not found", exactly as for a bookmark that does not exist.
Ask its owner to share it with you.
